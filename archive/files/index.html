Heritage Highlight

The Harbour Road House still stands — and still gathers the family

Built in 1923 and restored in 2019, the family home on Harbour Road remains the site of every Renaissance gathering. Its rooms hold three generations of portraits, correspondence and the family's original ledgers, now being catalogued into this archive.

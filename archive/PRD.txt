1. Executive Summary
Product Name

The Lutterodt Renaissance
A Digital Legacy for Present and Future Generations.

Vision

To build a world-class digital home for the Lutterodt family that preserves heritage, celebrates achievements, and connects generations through modern technology.

The MVP focuses on creating a premium public-facing experience that establishes credibility while laying the technical foundation for future member services, genealogy tools, AI-assisted archives, and governance systems.

13. Acceptance Criteria

The MVP will be considered complete when:

Visitors can easily understand the vision and history of the Lutterodt family.
Historical content can be published and maintained through the CMS.
The timeline, gallery, and family tree are fully functional in read-only mode.
The website performs well on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
The platform meets accessibility and security baselines.
Administrators can manage content without developer intervention.
14. Future-Proofing Considerations

Although the MVP is intentionally focused, the architecture should anticipate future expansion. Key design principles include:

Modular architecture: Features such as member dashboards, AI services, and philanthropy should plug into the existing system without major rewrites.
API-first approach: Expose data through well-defined APIs to support future mobile apps and integrations.
Scalable data model: Model people, events, media, and relationships so they can evolve into a full genealogy and knowledge graph.
Role-based access control: Implement permissions from the outset to simplify future member-only functionality.
Content-first strategy: Historical records, media, and stories should be stored with rich metadata to support future AI-assisted search and storytelling.

This Phase 1 MVP establishes a polished, production-ready foundation while positioning The Lutterodt Renaissance to evolve into a comprehensive digital legacy platform over subsequent phases
